5. Scoop: Senate GOP to send $5m to shore up Graham in South Carolina.

Photo illustration: Sarah Grillo/Axios. Photo: Sam Wolfe/Bloomberg via Getty Images

The main super PAC supporting Republican Senate candidates plans to inject $5 million into the South Carolina campaign as an "insurance policy" to help GOP nominee Darline Graham , a source familiar with the move tells Axios.

Why it matters: The GOP's concern that a Senate seat in blood-red South Carolina could be in play shows just how bad the political environment is for Republicans, who face a rash of troubles in states long considered conservative strongholds.

The shift to spending in conservative South Carolina comes after Senate Republicans announced they no longer were financing ads in swing-state North Carolina, where the GOP's Michael Whatley is lagging behind former Gov. Roy Cooper (D) in polls.
