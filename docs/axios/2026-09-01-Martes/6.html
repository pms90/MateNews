Vice President Vance on Monday offered an olive branch to Jewish Republicans who have grown wary of his views toward Israel.

Why it matters: Vance's appearance at the meeting is the latest indication that he's trying to repair ties with a powerful but skeptical constituency within the GOP ahead of an expected bid for president in 2028.

The background: Tensions between Vance and the pro-Israel conservatives have been growing for months.
