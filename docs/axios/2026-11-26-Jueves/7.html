Top AI executives are increasingly pushing back against a common view inside Anthropic: that humanity should surrender some decision-making power to superintelligent AI systems, sources told Axios.

Why it matters: The debate has picked up steam as top AI companies develop models certain employees consider too powerful to ever release . Many see Anthropic as the first company that will confront these questions due to its lead at the frontier of AI development.

Flashback: A product vision slide from several years ago that was revealed during litigation described Anthropic's "vision for a better world" as a "utopia ruled by god in a box."

The big picture: The thinking for Amodei and others is that once AI can demonstrably cure diseases, safeguard humans from harming themselves or prevent disasters better than any person, refusing to hand over control becomes irrational — or even unethical.

Driving the news: Rival executives are increasingly rebuking the notion that humans will surrender agency, even as major math and reasoning breakthroughs convince insiders that superintelligence is imminent.
