5. Exclusive poll: GOP's gains among Latinos fade in House battlegrounds.

Democrats have gained sharply with Latino voters in some of the country's most competitive House districts since 2024, a new Hart Research/TelevisaUnivision poll shared exclusively with Axios shows.

Why it matters: President Trump came close to erasing Democrats' longtime advantage among Latinos in the last presidential election. Now, with control of Congress at stake and Trump's approval ratings hitting new lows , that breakthrough appears to have eroded.

By the numbers: Democrats are gaining ground with Latino voters across a survey of 17 competitive House districts, opening a 58%-35% lead on the generic congressional ballot, according to the Hart Research for TelevisaUnivision poll.

Zoom in: In three Texas congressional districts — one on the Mexico border, another in suburban Fort Worth and another in the Austin-San Antonio corridor — Latino voters went from a self-reported 44-44 split in the 2024 presidential race to 56-36 in Democrats' favor today.

The intrigue: 11% of Latino voters who say they backed Trump in 2024 now support Democratic congressional candidates.

State of play: Overall, Trump is deeply underwater with Latino voters in these battleground districts.

What they're saying: " Latino voters aren't locked into one party. They're paying close attention and making decisions based on who shows up for them, and how they're showing up," TelevisaUnivision Senior Vice President Kate Coleman tells Axios.

Trump's immigration crackdown appears to have run into a wall with these voters — without producing a wholesale rejection of strict border enforcement.

Zoom out: The economy still outranks immigration as Latino voters' top concern, the survey indicates.

Methodology: The 2026 Latino Battleground survey included 1,500 Latino registered voters in 17 battleground congressional districts and was conducted Aug. 6-17, 2026, by Hart Research for TelevisaUnivision.
