5. Las encuestas económicas de Trump se hunden y rivalizan con las peores de Biden.

President Trump in the Oval Office. Photo: Anna Moneymaker/Getty Images

Americans overwhelmingly disapprove of President Trump's handling of the economy, reaching levels that rival some of the worst of former president Biden's term, per a new AP-NORC poll out Thursday.

Why it matters : The economy is stronger than sentiment suggests, but time is running short for the president and his administration to convince highly skeptical voters of that.

By the numbers: Just 26% of voters approve of Trump's handling of the economy overall, a fresh low, per the AP .

In mid-2022, at the worst of the post-COVID inflation, Biden's economic approval rating was 28%, AP noted.

Between the lines: The new poll fits with other national surveys over the last month that similarly showed Trump's approval on the economy somewhere from the high 20s to the high 30s.

The intrigue: There's ample evidence that the economy isn't that weak — growth is solid, the labor market is stable, and inflation is nowhere near the worst of Biden's era.

For the record: The White House did not immediately respond to a request for comment on the poll.

The bottom line: Trump won in 2024 promising to fix Biden's economy and make everything better.
