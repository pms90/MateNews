A Russian oligarch close to Vladimir Putin provided hundreds of thousands of dollars' worth of funding for Donald Trump Jr.'s May wedding in the Bahamas, according to a ProPublica investigation .

Why it matters: Umar Kremlev's deep ties to Vladimir Putin allegedly created a personal financial link between President Trump's oldest child and prominent Russians while the U.S. attempts to broker peace with Ukraine.

The ProPublica report outlines how Kremlev allegedly paid to rent one of the private islands that hosted the celebration for Trump Jr. and Bettina Anderson, as well as major costs including a fireworks show.

What they're saying: "It's unfortunate that something so personal and happy can be recast as something political or sinister simply because of who someone is or where they come from," Anderson wrote in an Instagram post Monday morning. "Friendship doesn't require a political motive."

Zoom out: The Kremlev-led IBA sought to influence Trump shortly after his 2025 inauguration with an open letter seeking help in its fight with the International Olympic Committee, ProPublica reported.

Kremlev, 43, developed ties with the Russian oligarchy in the early 2010s through his membership in the Night Wolves, a Russian motorcycle club that considers Putin an honorary member, the Washington Post reported .

In 2015 , Kremlev founded a boxing promotion organization called Patriot and facilitated a meeting between Putin and former American star Roy Jones Jr. that led to Jones gaining Russian citizenship.

Shortly after Kremlev took the reins in 2021, the IBA formed a partnership with Gazprom, Russia's state-controlled energy giant. The financial arrangement helped the IBA get out of debt.
