5. FlyDubai flight spirals into chaos. What we know and what remains a mystery.

A FlyDubai Boeing 737 MAX 8 approaches Ben Gurion Airport near Tel Aviv on Sept. 30. Photo: Jack Guez/AFP via Getty Images

A FlyDubai flight bound for Israel erupted into chaos after a pilot stabbed another pilot and sent the plane spiraling thousands of feet.

The big picture: The midair emergency triggered a multinational security response, but key details about what happened inside the cockpit remained unclear.

Driving the news: Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu said in a video statement Wednesday that the plane was forced to make an emergency landing in Saudi Arabia.

The passengers have now safely landed in Israel.

Here's what we know and don't know.

By the numbers: The Tel Aviv-bound plane left the United Arab Emirates on Sept. 30, per FlightAware .

What they're saying: Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz called the incident "an attempted jihadist terror attack" that was only stopped by "the heroism of several Israeli passengers," per multiple reports.

The Saudi-company Cluster2 , which manages the country's airports, said in a statement that the flight's captain and first officer were being treated for injuries at the hospital.

Passengers described frightening moments on board, telling the New York Times they feared the aircraft was about to crash.

Zoom out: Major questions remain about the cockpit incident, including the motive.

There are also conflicting reports about what happened inside the cockpit and why the plane suddenly plunged.

It's also unclear who landed the plane and how passengers got involved.

Yes, but: A FlyDubai spokesperson told the New York Times that the crew members themselves secured the aircraft. It's also unclear how many passengers got involved.

It's unclear how the weapon got aboard, too.
