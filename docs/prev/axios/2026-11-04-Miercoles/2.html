2. Democrats' new balancing act: Subpoenas and affordability.

President Trump speaks at a campaign rally at Five Flags Center on November 1, 2026 in Iowa. Photo: Andrew Harnik/Getty Images.

Democrats wasted no time gloating and warning the Trump administration about a pending flood of subpoenas and investigations once the 2026 midterm blue wave became clear.

Why it matters: Democrats are eager to exercise their oversight powers over the president and his allies, but they must navigate that while addressing Americans' No. 1 election concern — the high cost of living.

Driving the news: Before the AP even called the House for Democrats, expected House Oversight Chair Robert Garcia (D-Calif.) said in a statement that the president's allies should "[l]awyer up." He seconded the sentiment in a Wednesday post to X.

The big picture: Voters aren't necessarily asking Democrats to choose between the cost of living and accountability. They want action on both.

What they're saying: When asked about Wyden and Garcia's comments in an interview Wednesday morning, House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries (D-N.Y.) — the overwhelming favorite to become House Speaker — told TODAY that the "American people clearly don't want us to try and get even."

The other side: "As the President has said, he's willing to work with anyone to continue delivering victories for hardworking Americans," White House spokesperson Lauren Bis told Axios in an emailed statement.

Zoom in: Democrats have already laid out some of their main investigation priorities.

President Trump said on Monday he's open to pardoning his allies and staff if Democrats target them.

Reality check: Some legislative priorities Democrats want could also be easily blocked by the president's veto power. Democrats are not currently projected to win the 2/3 majority needed to override such a move on their own.

What we're watching: Whether Democrats truly pursue an effective oversight agenda and whether Democratic leadership can walk the tightrope of bringing costs down while doing so. Go deeper: Trump vows to protect Musk, floats pre-emptive staff pardons
