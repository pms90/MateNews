President Trump told Axios in a phone interview on Thursday that he isn't concerned that Russia will attack NATO countries.

Why it matters: European leaders have grown extremely concerned and warned publicly that Moscow could test NATO's resolve — and Trump's commitment to Article 5, the alliance's collective-defense clause — through a limited attack or other military provocations.

What he's saying: "I am not concerned ... at all. There is no problem," Trump said when asked about a possible Russian provocation against NATO countries.

The other side: Naryshkin, the director of the Russian foreign intelligence service, confirmed on Thursday that he met with Ratcliffe during his visit to Moscow.

State of play: Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky told Axios in July that Putin is planning a massive mobilization of 500,000 troops in the coming months for a renewed offensive in Ukraine.
