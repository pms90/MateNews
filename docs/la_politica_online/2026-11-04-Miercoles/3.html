Vance y Rubio quedaron golpeados en las elecciones y se abre la carrera por la sucesión de Trump

El vicepresidente y el secretario de Estado quedaron afectados en sus aspiraciones para la Casa Blanca.

El colapso electoral de los republicanos este martes, en la elección de medio termino, tiene implicancias directas en la sucesión de Donald Trump en 2028: tanto el vicepresidente JD Vance como el secretario de Estado Marco Rubio quedaron afectados en sus aspiraciones para la Casa Blanca.

Vance emitió un mensaje en la red social X en el cual realizó una profunda autocrítica por la derrota electoral. Desde ya, culpó a la guerra en Medio Oriente y buscó exhibir coherencia: el vicepresidente no aplaudió la captura de Nicolás Maduro en enero de este año, nunca mostró afinidad con Israel y chocó con Volodomir Zelenski en público, en una reunión en la Casa Blanca, en febrero del año pasado.

One of the things the data analysts on our side obsessed over was "composition"-whether the electorate looked close enough to 2024 for Republicans to win. And despite polls that showed a Democrat blowout, the data kept on telling us the same story: in some states turnout was merely OK, in other states it was downright amazing, and in no state was it catastrophic. There was a genuine sense of optimism on our team: our people were showing up. So, I asked the smartest data guy I know, does that mean we're going to shock the world? "No," he told me. "We might do better than the polls suggest. But the data is telling us that a lot of Trump/Vance 2024 voters are pissed and planning to vote for the other guys. Our voters are turning out, but they may not vote for us." Well, that looks prophetic now. Our voters did show up. In Florida, for example, the electorate was 9 points more Republican than Democrat, just a couple points worse than 2024. Undoubtedly there were places we could have done better on turnout. But the main problem is not turnout. The main problem is that many people who voted for Trump/Vance in 2024 voted for Democrats in 2026. There will be a lot of infighting and arguing over what happened and why that is. One of the pollsters I really respect told me that our economic messaging wasn't sharp enough down the stretch. I'm sure that's true. But the real reason we lost is obvious to almost everyone. Another pollster-one of the smartest people I know in politics, told me yesterday we'd lose the generic ballot by 13 points. I felt negative going into Tuesday, but even I was shocked by this. In his polling, he saw that a lot of Trump 2024 voters were angry and planning to vote for Democrats. He asked those voters who had abandoned us what would cause them to come back on our team. The top four answers, accounting for nearly all of the responses were: 1) End the war 2) Focus on cost of living/inflation 3) Put America First 4) Change our relationship to Israel Our voters showed up. And the above four reasons are why many of them voted for Democrats. This is according to a pollster who nailed the results. While many spent Monday attacking Rachel Campos Duffy for calling for a diplomatic resolution to the conflict, it turns out her political advice was sound. (She's also a great person.) In the next few months, there's going to be a lot of gaslighting. Expect to hear any number of establishment politicians say the solution to our woes is amnesty. I'm open to the idea that we should change enforcement-by focusing more on businesses that hire illegal labor, for instance. But anyone pushing amnesty is lying to you. The reason we lost is the war. The president himself said so last night. If you'd like to argue it was worth it, that losing the Senate for the next 6 years was worth it, then do so. Argue it was a worthwhile cost to pay. Agree or disagree, that's at least honest. But everyone knows why we lost. And any honest conversation about where we go from here must start there. I remain hopeful about our country. To the people who are pissed off and want to say, "I told you so": you're right, you did tell us so. Take that energy into electing Republicans who share your worldview. To the people who are just pissed at the results, you should be. Let's take that anger and get back to the work of saving our country.

Más allá de la narrativa, la base MAGA que respaldaba al presidente fue arrasada en la noche del martes. Los republicanos perdieron en el estado de Vance, Ohio, donde la banca de senador del vicepresidente será ocupada por el senador Sherrod Brown

Vance se involucró, además, en la campaña perdedora de Texas, donde abrazó al candidato al Senado Ken Paxton que tenía un apoyo económico récord de Ellon Musk.

Los demócratas podrían llevarse el triunfo, en números finales, en más de 300 distritos electorales y de los diez estados con mayor cantidad de habitantes solo uno será republicano: Florida.

El gobernador saliente de dicha entidad, Ron De Santis, otro alfil del mundo MAGA, emitió un video en las últimas horas en el cual reconoció que para los republicanos será muy complicado recuperar el Senado en "al menos seis años".

Rubio también termino golpeado. Si bien la guerra en Medio Oriente se le atribuye mayoritariamente al secretario de Defensa Pete Hegseth, el responsable de toda la política exterior de Trump es Rubio.

Por estas horas, el mensaje dominante al interior del Partido Republicano es que las elecciones se perdieron por haber dedicado demasiada energía a cuestiones externas que implicaron un descuido de la economía.

En los últimos meses, además, Rubio había realizado diversos esfuerzos para ser aceptado por la base de su partido, una trayectoria que se inició el año pasado cuando desmanteló todos los programas de apoyo multilateral.

Al secretario de Estado le queda como consuelo que su partido no perdió Florida y poco más porque, a diferencia de Vance, tampoco hizo demasiada campaña en los estados.

Para el presidente el escenario sucesorio es complejo. Le queda en su favor una gran capacidad de recaudación electoral pero también la realidad de que los senadores republicanos elegidos anoche trascenderán su mandato y, por lo tanto, no estarán obligado a cerrar filas con la Casa Blanca.
