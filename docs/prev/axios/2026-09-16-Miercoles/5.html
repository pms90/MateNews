5. "Simplify" your life.

Cover: Crown Currency

Our new book — "Simplify: Do 50% More with 50% Less" — is out Tuesday. Please order it ! The content has been literally life-changing for us.

We think our lives are unavoidably complicated. Nope.

Why it matters: We're to blame. We clog up our minds and days through bad habits, bad decisions to say yes instead of no, and an odd refusal to think more deeply about how we spend our time.

The big picture: The numbers capture the urgency. You get 168 hours a week. Sleep takes 50–60. Work takes 40+. Getting ready, commuting and complaining about work take another 20. That leaves about 50 hours you kinda, sorta control.

We discovered the only way to shift this, both at home and at work, was to create a new mindset or personal operating system. We came up with a simple formula for making choices: Confront, Delete, Amplify.

But you need to start with half an hour of reflection about what you truly want out of life.

Let me share an example from my life:

So I made the conscious decision to say no to almost any social engagement related to work, even though there's a lot of pressure as a media CEO to be public-facing. I also decided to focus on a very few friendships instead of seeking new ones. Think about the countless hours this frees up.

Start by looking at your closet. We accumulate so much stuff, which requires us to spend time sorting and thinking, when most people wear only a few things. That might seem trivial, but it literally drains our brains.

If you think we don't have an epidemic of too much crap, sit with this: There are nearly 10 times as many self-storage centers in America as hospitals. We're paying rent on things we'll never touch again.

The bottom line: " Simplify" walks you through a dozen or so ways to confront what you waste time doing, how to delete it, then how to amplify the stuff that makes us feel good.
