Harbin park unpacks ice blocks as it readies for largest-ever winter show.

The 28th Harbin Ice and Snow World began construction on Tuesday afternoon, as insulation covers were removed from stored ice blocks, revealing crystal-clear ice ready to be carved for the winter attraction in Harbin, Heilongjiang province.

"A total of 200,000 cubic meters of ice blocks had been carefully stored for more than 10 months," said Qiao Haibo, director of the engineering management department of Harbin Ice and Snow World Co. "After undergoing processes such as inspection, cutting and shaping, the ice blocks will be used to build the main tower, the super slide and the Disney Frozen-themed area."

As artificial ice often contains air bubbles and is not sufficiently strong for carving, the park uses ice harvested from the frozen Songhua River.

Combining traditional ice storage methods with modern multilayer insulation technology, the company has created suitable storage conditions featuring low temperatures, protection from light, insulation and sealing.

These measures ensure that the ice remains frozen even when temperatures exceed 30 C during the peak of summer.

Once the Songhua River freezes completely and the ice reaches the required thickness, more ice will be harvested from the river to ensure an adequate supply of high-quality materials for this winter's construction projects.

According to the carving specifications, each ice block measures about 80 centimeters wide, 40 cm thick and 160 cm long.

The park, which combines ice and snow landscapes with sound and light effects, will set a new record this winter, expanding its area from 1.2 million square meters last winter to 1.25 million sq m.

A total of 400,000 cubic meters of ice and snow will be used in its construction.

More than 1,000 ice-and-snow structures are expected to be built across six major sections covering ice-and-snow landscapes, amusement experiences, performances, ice-and-snow competitions, themed intellectual property and supporting services, offering visitors an immersive winter experience.

Iconic landmarks, including West Lake in Hangzhou, Zhejiang province, and Tengwang Pavilion in Nanchang, Jiangxi province, will be recreated in ice sculptures.

The company has also announced a collaboration with Disney China.

The 28th Harbin Ice and Snow World will feature a themed area incorporating characters and story elements from Disney's Frozen franchise.

Visitors will be able to admire ice sculptures of Elsa, Anna and Olaf, alongside themed ice-and-snow landscapes and interactive experiences.

The park will also offer 100 ice slides this winter, including a new 400-meter-long attraction featuring a 20-meter drop and seven parallel lanes. Another 20 small and medium-sized ice-and-snow slides will cater to visitors of different ages.

While the longer slides will allow young visitors to race down the slopes at high speeds, shorter, gentler slides will be suitable for children, providing entertainment for the whole family.

Other activities will include skiing, cross-country skiing, ice skating, ice cycling, ice mazes and ice spinning tops, ensuring visitors of different ages and interests can find something to enjoy.
