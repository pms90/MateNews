DPP criticized for cultural manipulation and suppression of expression.

More and more people in Taiwan have expressed deep concern over the political manipulation of the island's cultural environment by "Taiwan independence" separatist forces, a Chinese mainland spokesman said on Wednesday.

Chen Binhua, spokesman for the State Council Taiwan Affairs Office, made the remarks in response to recent comments by veteran Taiwan singer and songwriter Lo Ta-yu about the Democratic Progressive Party authorities' restrictions on freedom of expression on the island.

Lo, 72, criticized the DPP authorities in a recent radio interview, saying they had become a source of fear among Taiwan residents and contributed to a decline in the island's cultural environment. He also urged people in Taiwan to "awaken to the situation and speak out for the next generation". His remarks have sparked widespread discussion on both sides of the Taiwan Strait.

Chen said Lo's comments reflected the severity of the problem, as the singer spoke out about the cultural turmoil in Taiwan society and expressed deep concern over the situation.

"An increasing number of Taiwan compatriots have expressed strong dissatisfaction with the DPP authorities' actions that undermine democracy and run counter to public opinion," Chen said.

Highlighting the openness and inclusiveness that have characterized Chinese culture since ancient times, Chen accused the DPP authorities of promoting cultural "Taiwan independence" for their own political interests and creating an atmosphere of "green terror" and a "chilling effect" on the island, instilling fear among Taiwan residents.

Chen said these actions had seriously undermined the interests of Taiwan compatriots and sparked strong dissatisfaction and widespread opposition among the people.
