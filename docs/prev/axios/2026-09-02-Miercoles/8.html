"Like pissing in the wind": Local concerns bulldozed by Trump's border wall plan.

Photo illustration: Sarah Grillo/Axios. Photos: Brandon Bell, Kent Nishimura/AFP, and Kaylee Greenlee/Bloomberg via Getty Images

The Trump administration has a blunt message for state and local officials trying to prevent construction of a wall and other security measures along the 2,000-mile southern border: Resistance is futile.

Driving the news: President Trump's envoys recently met privately with officials from Texas border counties where residents have pushed back against construction, saying the plans are unnecessary in rugged landscapes with few immigrant crossings and that they'd harm the environment.

Zoom in: Scott told local officials that wall construction along a more than 200-mile stretch from Hudspeth County to Presidio County was "not negotiable" without giving a reason, Brewster County Sheriff Ronny Dodson, who was at the meeting, told Axios.

At the meeting , Scott acknowledged a lack of transparency by the federal government on construction plans so far, Dodson said.

The latest: A federal judge issued a temporary restraining order pausing construction over the roughly 600 miles of border, but that order lasts only through the end of this month.

What they're saying: " We are currently evaluating technology options to meet President Trump's mission of zero illegal border crossings while strengthening the most secure border in American history in highly remote and hard-to-access areas," a CBP spokesperson said in a statement.

The intrigue: Federal officials are giving Brewster County, home to much of the Big Bend national and state parks as well as the Black Gap Wildlife Management area, some room to negotiate.

Dodson said he's been taking local CBP officials on site visits to try to persuade them from building a new road through the parks and limit the number of vehicle barriers CBP plans.

The big picture: The pressure on local officials in the Big Bend area to submit to the construction plans is part of a pattern for CBP, which had built less than 200 miles of border wall as of last week.
