Beidou sees growing use across industries.

Daily service calls exceed 100 million as applications gain wider traction

China's Beidou Navigation Satellite System has become deeply integrated into the daily lives of Chinese people, receiving more than 100 million service calls from across the country each day.

According to a Beidou industry development report published on Tuesday by the Global Navigation Satellite System and Location-Based Services Association of China, as of the first half of 2026, the number of various types of Beidou terminal devices in operation across China — excluding consumer electronics — stood at nearly 42 million units.

The figure represents an increase of nearly 9 million units from the same period last year, or a year-on-year growth of about 27 percent.

Of the total, more than 38 million Beidou terminal units were deployed across 14 key industrial sectors, posting year-on-year growth of more than 26 percent.

The report also said that Beidou applications have continued to expand in the mass consumer market.

In the first half of 2026, about 124 million smartphones were produced and placed on the domestic market, more than 98 percent of which support Beidou services.

During the same period, approximately 10 million passenger vehicles equipped with Beidou terminals were put on the Chinese market, while sales of electric bicycles with Beidou positioning capabilities totaled 26 million units, according to the report.

In terms of applications, transportation remains firmly in the top tier, followed by telecommunications, natural resources development, emergency response and agriculture.

Beidou services have also been growing steadily in finance, the medical industry and healthcare, with particularly notable growth in the petroleum and petrochemical sector, the report said.

Zhang Huifeng, president of the association, said that over the coming years, Beidou will evolve from a navigation system into a unified spatiotemporal service infrastructure underpinning China's digital economy and the broader economy.

"Driven by deep integration with 5G and 6G technologies, artificial intelligence, the internet of things and low-Earth orbit satellites, an integrated space-air-ground spatiotemporal service system is taking shape at an accelerated pace. Demand for Beidou services will keep growing across many business fields, including low-altitude air transportation, high-level intelligent driving, smart agriculture, digital twins and embodied intelligence," he said at a Beidou services forum in Beijing on Tuesday.

In the global market, Zhang said, Beidou will shift its focus from terminal hardware sales to the integrated delivery of data, platforms and services.

Beidou is China's national navigation and positioning satellite network and one of the four global navigation satellite systems, alongside the United States' GPS, Russia's GLONASS and the European Union's Galileo.

Since 2000, 64 Beidou satellites have been launched on 47 Long March 3-series rockets from the Xichang Satellite Launch Center in Sichuan province. The system was declared complete in July 2020 and began providing full-scale global services.

Currently, more than 50 Beidou satellites are in active service, operating in multiple orbits.

According to the association, the overall value of satellite-enabled navigation and positioning services in China was about 629 billion yuan ($94.37 billion) by the end of 2025, representing a 9.24 percent increase year-on-year.
