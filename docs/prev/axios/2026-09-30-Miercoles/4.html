4. U.K. PM: "Strong indications" Iran had role in bomb plot.

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham at the Labour Party Conference in Liverpool, England, on Wednesday. Photo: Peter Byrne/PA Images via Getty Images

U.K. Prime Minister Andy Burnham said there are "strong indications that Iran played a part" in a suspected bomb plot at a British air base used by the U.S.

The big picture: Burnham's comments to the BBC come after Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Monday that the weekend incident at RAF Fairford "clearly involves the hands ‌of a foreign actor." Tehran denies any involvement.

What they're saying: "We will say more in due course but we can confirm ... that we believe Iran to have played a part," Burnham told the BBC, declining to elaborate.

Context: U.K. earlier this year allowed the U.S. Air Force to use RAF Fairford for limited defensive strikes against Iranian missile capabilities.
